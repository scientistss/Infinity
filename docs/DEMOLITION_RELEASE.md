## Delivered scope and compatibility

The [project-specific rules](DEMOLITION.md) support one explicitly confirmed, paid, single-level demolition of an ordinary building on its fixed planet. The work has its own stable paid identity and saved duration. Completion releases one occupied field; cancellation before completion refunds the actual demolition fee. Completing demolition does not refund the original construction cost. Rebuilding remains a separate ordinary paid action.

Ordinary construction, research, shipyard, missile capacity, finite plans, templates and enabled conflicting protocol cards retain their stated interlocks. A locked protocol slot can be stopped or cleared without first upgrading robotics. Its configuration is not silently removed or enabled again. Warehouse downsizing retains excess stock and prevents further production above capacity. Unrepresentable positive payments/refunds fail atomically with a precision explanation, without consuming resources or an ID.

This is not bulk or automatic demolition. Terraformers, moon facilities and unopened facilities remain excluded. No field-revocation ledger or NPC economy is introduced. Existing terraformer grants and old paid work retain their prior semantics.

Historical r10 migration adds an explicit null demolition slot to each planet, plus the envelope revision, after the existing old-reader normalization. It creates no demolition job or spending permission. Startup can also legitimately apply the existing offline engine to the independently observed elapsed interval; that time settlement must be distinguished from format migration. Original raw bytes are protected by the existing backup/readback/failure contract.
